EMAILJS TEMPLATE SETUP — copy these values into the EmailJS template editor
============================================================================

Where: EmailJS dashboard -> Email Templates -> Create New Template

------------------------------------------------------------
Settings tab
------------------------------------------------------------
To Email:      your new email address (the inbox you want messages in)
From Name:     {{name}}
Reply To:      {{email}}
Subject:       New message from {{name}} - Portfolio Contact Form

------------------------------------------------------------
Content tab (template body)
------------------------------------------------------------
You have a new message from your portfolio site.

Name: {{name}}
Email: {{email}}

Message:
{{message}}

------------------------------------------------------------
After saving
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy the "Template ID" shown at the top of the template page
   -> paste into email.js as PUBLIC_CONFIG.templateId

2. Go to Email Services -> copy the "Service ID" of the service
   you connected to your new inbox
   -> paste into email.js as PUBLIC_CONFIG.serviceId

3. Go to Account -> General -> copy your "Public Key"
   -> paste into email.js as PUBLIC_CONFIG.publicKey

The variable names above ({{name}}, {{email}}, {{message}}) must match
exactly — those are the field names the contact form on the site sends.
